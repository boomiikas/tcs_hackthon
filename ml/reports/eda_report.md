## 5. Strategic Insights for Optimization

1. **Weekend and Vacation Dropoff**: Friday and weekend demand varies significantly by food category, requiring differentiated buffers.
2. **Core Staples vs Specialty Waste**: High-waste culprits tend to be specialty items with volatile demand; CanteenIQ preparation planning should employ tighter buffers for these items.
3. **Waste Reduction Target**: Eliminating half of avoidable waste unlocks over INR 6,000,000+ in annual canteen contribution.
